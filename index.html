Automatic publishing: Not available yet

This app currently searches public listings and saves plans in this browser. It does not yet have a server-side publishing queue, cloud media storage, or approved publishing connections. The computer being off is only solved after those server services are live.

Platform constraints: Facebook Pages can be published through Meta's approved Pages API; arbitrary Facebook Groups cannot. TikTok, Instagram professional accounts, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest and Telegram each require their own approved app permissions and account authorization. X requires its own API access.

These controls are disabled deliberately; enabling them before the publishing services exist would falsely claim posts will be sent.
